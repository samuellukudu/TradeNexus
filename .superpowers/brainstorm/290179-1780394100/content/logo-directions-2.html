More Icon Directions — Part 2

Expanded set of mark concepts for TradeNexus. Click any that catch your eye.

E — Globe + Nexus

World map with connected nodes. International trade meets network hub. Globe says "global," nodes say "AI-powered connections."

F — Interlocking Shapes

Two shapes interlocking = supplier meets buyer, East meets West. Simple, bold, memorable. Works at all sizes.

G — Bridge / Connect

A bridge linking two sides. Direct metaphor for connecting suppliers to buyers across borders. Warm and optimistic.

H — Hexagon Tech

Hexagonal network with data nodes. The honeycomb shape suggests structure, intelligence, and platform. Very SaaS.

I — Lettermark "TN"

Bold typographic mark. T + N in a chip/card shape with a subtle AI spark. Clean, direct, unmistakable.

J — Compass / Navigator

A compass rose pointing in all directions. "We guide you to the right markets." Suggests discovery, exploration, precision.

K — Overlapping Rings / Venn

Two overlapping circles creating a shared center. Supplier + Buyer = Deal. Classic matchmaking visual. Simple and universal.

L — Pulse / Signal

Radiating waves from a center point. Like a signal broadcasting — "we find leads for you." Sonar, radar, AI scanning the market.

M — Infinity / Endless Trade

A continuous loop suggesting ongoing relationships, repeat business, and the endless cycle of global commerce. Elegant and fluid.

N — Diamond / Value

Faceted diamond shape with internal connections. "We find hidden value." Premium, global, structured. Could feel too luxury.

O — Bidirectional Arrows

Two arrows converging in a center point. Imports meet exports. Supply meets demand. Direct, unmistakable trade symbolism.

P — Constellation / Star Map

Nodes connected across a map, with one arrow pointing outward. "We map your next market." Exploratory, intelligent, data-driven.
