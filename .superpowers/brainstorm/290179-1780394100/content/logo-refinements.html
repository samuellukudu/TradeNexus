Bridge / Connect — 6 Variations

G1 — Classic Arch

Clean arch bridge. Three anchor points. Bold, simple, unmistakable silhouette.

G2 — Block + Span

Solid pillars with a spanning cable. More grounded, structural. Suggests infrastructure and reliability.

G3 — Dual Bridge

Two parallel arcs — supplier and buyer, connected at a shared center. More dynamic, layered depth.

G4 — Suspension

Suspension bridge with cable stays. Towers = market entry points. More architectural and distinctive.

G5 — Data Bridge

Arching bridge with data flow dots. Blends "connection" with "intelligence." The dots suggest information moving.

G6 — Orbital Bridge

Two worlds connected by orbiting paths. Global trade made simple. Clean circular form — works beautifully as favicon.

Pulse / Signal — 6 Variations

L1 — Classic Sonar

Concentric rings with upward arrow at center. "We scan the market, find opportunity." Clean radar aesthetic.

L2 — Radar Sweep

Active scanning with a found target. "We found your next lead." Story-driven, more dynamic than static rings.

L3 — Upward Waves

Expanding arcs moving upward with an arrow. Growth + discovery. Optimistic, forward-moving energy.

L4 — Dashed Rings + TN

Dotted concentric rings with "TN" at center. Pairs the lettermark with the signal motif. More brand-forward.

L5 — Crosshair / Target

Precision targeting with acquired lead. "We find exactly the right buyers." Crosshair + tracking. Very distinctive.

L6 — Network Pulse

Pulse rings with connected outer nodes. Blends the "nexus" network concept with the "pulse" signal. Best of both.
