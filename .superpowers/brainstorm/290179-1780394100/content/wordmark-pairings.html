How the orbital bridge icon pairs with "TradeNexus" text. Pick the layout that feels right.

A — Horizontal (icon left)

Classic logo lockup. Best for headers, nav bars, and the app sidebar. Most common B2B SaaS pattern.

B — Stacked (icon above)

Icon-first, text beneath. Better for square spaces — landing page hero, social avatars, app store icons.

Trade Nexus

C — Inline (icon as divider)

Icon sits between "Trade" and "Nexus" like a connector. Bold conceptual move — the mark is the nexus between trade partners.

TradeNexus

D — Icon-dominant

Large mark, small wordmark. For spaces where the icon is the hero — app icon, favicon, PWA, social profile pics.

💡 You don't have to pick just one. A brand system uses different lockups for different contexts: horizontal for the nav bar, stacked for the landing page, icon-only for the favicon. The question is: which is the primary/default lockup?
