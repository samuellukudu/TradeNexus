Decision 1: How Many Use Cases?

A

All Three Cases

Feature Fiji HVAC, Australia Excavators, and Saudi Solar. Shows product versatility across industries and regions. More content = more proof.

B

One Hero Case + Two Summary

Lead with Australia excavators as the main case study (best data, most relatable), then show Fiji and Saudi as secondary cards. Focuses the narrative.

Decision 2: How To Present The Data

A

Live Data Cards

Show actual lead cards from the CSV — company name, confidence %, match reasoning. Like a "preview" of what you get. Feels real and tangible.

B

Stats & Numbers

"14 verified buyers found in 60 seconds" with aggregate metrics. Cleaner, but less tangible. Better for scanning.

C

Hybrid: Stats + Sample Cards

Big number headline + 2-3 sample lead cards below. Best of both: scannable stat with tangible proof right after.
