# Viral Video Strategy — Approach A: Voiceover-First Repackaging

**Date:** 2026-06-03
**Status:** Approved
**Topic:** Make TradeNexus short-form video content go viral by adding narrative voiceover to existing rendered videos

## Problem

TradeNexus has multiple rendered marketing videos with a distinctive "Terminal Nexus" aesthetic (dark, data-heavy, monospace, Geist headlines). Posted across WeChat Channels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts with background music but no voiceover. Performance is stuck at ~300 views (WeChat) and ~100 views (TikTok/YouTube). The content isn't breaking through because:

- **No voiceover = no narrative.** Viewers see abstract terminal visuals without understanding what's happening or why they should care.
- **The "hidden opportunity cost" hook is intellectual, not emotional.** It requires prior understanding of the problem. Viral hooks trigger emotion in 1-2 seconds.
- **Music alone can't explain a B2B product.** It sets mood but carries zero information. The viewer has no reason to keep watching.
- **No URL in captions means the video IS the funnel.** It must create enough urgency that viewers search for TradeNexus or DM.

## Goal

Increase per-video views to minimum 1,000 (WeChat), 5,000 (TikTok), 2,000 (YouTube Shorts) within 30 days. Drive measurable DMs/comments per video. Build awareness that leads suppliers to search for and sign up to TradeNexus.

## Design: 3-Act Voiceover Structure

Every video follows the same arc. 30-60 seconds:

### Act 1: HOOK (0-5 seconds)
One sentence that stops the scroll. Delivered in the first 1-2 seconds, before visuals fully resolve. Must trigger emotion: curiosity, FOMO, disbelief, or pain.

### Act 2: PROOF (5-45 seconds)
The AI doing the work. Terminal visuals, data reveals, counters ticking up, regions highlighted on the globe. Voiceover explains what's happening in plain conversational language — the way you'd explain it to a factory owner at lunch, not a board presentation.

### Act 3: PAYOFF + CTA (45-60 seconds)
The result stated clearly. One search instruction: "Search TradeNexus" (English) or "微信搜TradeNexus" / "私信'出海'" (Chinese). No URL — just a memorable, searchable name.

## Audio Design

### Layer Stack (top to bottom)
1. **Voiceover track** — primary content. Recorded clean, normalized to -3dB peak.
2. **Background music** — secondary. Duck to -20dB during VO. Full volume only during intro and transition moments (between acts).
3. **Sound design accents** — subtle, optional: terminal keystroke clicks when data appears, a soft "ping" when a lead is found, a low whoosh on region transitions. Sparse — 3-5 accents per video max.

### Music Selection
Match the Terminal Nexus palette: dark synthwave, ambient techno, or driving cinematic. Steady pulse preferred — avoid tracks with sudden changes or vocals that fight the VO. Sources: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or royalty-free synthwave collections.

### VO Recording
- **Chinese content (WeChat, 抖音):** Record yourself. Authentic accent builds trust with Chinese supplier audience. Phone mic is acceptable if recorded in a quiet room at ~15cm distance.
- **English content (TikTok, YouTube Shorts):** You record OR use ElevenLabs for fast iteration. Authentic human voice preferred if quality is good.

## VO Script Principles

- **Conversational, not corporate.** Imagine explaining to a factory owner at lunch.
- **Every line must earn its screen time.** If a sentence doesn't create desire for the next sentence, cut it.
- **No jargon.** "Verified leads" not "multi-vector parallel search squads."
- **Numbers are your best friend.** "47 buyers in Germany" beats "many buyers globally."
- **Match the visual rhythm.** When the terminal shows data appearing, VO should describe what just appeared. When the globe shows a new region highlighted, VO should name it.
